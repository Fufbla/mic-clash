HOW TO PLAY

Land punches, slaps, and kicks to fill your special meter. When it glows, unleash your fighter’s special. Specials cannot be blocked. Your fighter is protected until the full special finishes; new attacks wait until then.

CONTROLLERS
Left stick / D-pad: move · A / ×: jump (tap again for double jump) · X / □: punch or slap · B / ○: kick · Y / △: special · shoulder / trigger: block · Menu / Options: pause. In Local 2P, press A / × on each controller to join, pick with ← / →, then press A / × again to ready. Both ready starts the round. Either controller can navigate pause and rematch menus.

Drag the left stick to move. Use the four round buttons to jump, punch, kick, or use your special. For Get It, Dr. No Bunnies, Smack Prince, MooJa, Ocrrrrr!, and Sassy T, the Slap button delivers an open-hand slap. For Quick Fast, the Headbutt button strikes with his head and Kick performs a backflip kick. For Can't Rap, the Punch button performs a microphone strike. Tap Jump again for a double jump; Get It, Loud Bull, FatBoi, Munchy, and HeHaw use normal double jumps, while Karate Fighter and Snitchy flip. Rotate your phone to play in portrait or landscape. Only the Pause button or a controller’s Menu / Options button pauses a round. Each fighter starts with 320 health.

Rapid Punches switches to a white gi. Call the Cops brings a police car and three officers. Get It’s Live Performance pulls out a microphone and sends singing waves toward the opponent, ending in a cartoon explosion. Loud Bull walks with a bob-and-weave guard. His Giant Yell makes his mouth huge and blasts the opponent with sound. FatBoi waddles as he walks. For Bite & Toss, he bites a chicken wing first, then throws the bitten wing to damage his opponent. Munchy walks in a fighting guard. Munch grabs a nearby opponent for three giant-mouth cartoon chomps, then releases them. Stay close and catch them on the ground. Dr. No Bunnies uses the African Pride Belt Slap: move close, then land three protected, unblockable 16-damage strikes. “LASHES!” appears on each strike, and the third launches the opponent backward. HeHaw points forward to call Horse Power: a black stallion gallops across the stage and knocks her opponent backward. Can't Rap calls in eight small red-turban crew members who rush the opponent for eight protected, unblockable cartoon hits. Smack Prince dances as he moves. The Smack Upgrade puts him in a tuxedo beneath a bright spotlight, then delivers a powerful open-hand slap that sends a nearby opponent flying. Move close before activating. Baby Big Fists grows enormous fists and throws two punches, with an “mmm” speech bubble on each punch. The second hit launches the opponent backward. His fists shrink back to normal afterward. MooJa transforms into a cow and fires three milk missiles from her mouth, then changes back. Jump over the missiles to evade them. Cranes walks elegantly and lets out an angry shout to summon Elevator Drop. Move out of the glowing landing area before the elevator falls; jumping in place will not avoid it. Demon points at his opponent, then looks away and up. The Crew rushes in from both sides and drops from above, landing a twelve-hit combo and launching the opponent backward. Move away from the marked area before they attack. Ocrrrrr! holds out both hands for Nail Pierce. Her pink nails grow toward the opponent, land one powerful piercing hit, then shrink back. Stay beyond their reach or double-jump over them.

The President calls Executive Airstrike: a jet flies over and drops a cartoon bomb onto the marked area. Move out of the landing circle to evade it. O’whinny raises her arms for Money Rain: four waves of cash bundles pile onto the marked area. RoboKing calls Cybertruck Protocol: his silver truck charges in, then his robot hops out and punches the opponent before leaving. The President’s bodyguard and RoboKing’s robot follow them and automatically block every tenth incoming basic attack, with no damage or special-meter gain for the attacker. Misses and specials do not count; specials bypass Personal Security. Quick Fast charges through his rival with Quick Fast Rush. Sassy T opens his purse and throws slime while shouting “Sliiiime!” when sound is enabled. J Minors panics and flees while his pursuer swings a handbag at the opponent. Jump over the rush or slime, or move out of the chase strike to evade.
